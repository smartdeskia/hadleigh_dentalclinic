Hygiene Plus

Direct access hygiene

The dental hygienist at Hadleigh Dental & Cosmetic Centre treats patients from Thundersley, Rayleigh, South Benfleet, and the surrounding area — no need to be an existing patient. New patients are accepted on a “direct access” basis, meaning hygienist visits are possible without joining the practice or becoming a member.

from £52.00 (30 min visit)

What does a dental hygienist do?

  • Performs a “scale and polish” — a professional deep clean removing tartar, helping prevent gum disease
  • Identifies problem areas and shares tips to manage them
  • Recommends interdental devices for healthier teeth and gums
  • Teaches good oral health habits for long-term benefit
  • Helps prevent tooth decay between dentist visits
  • Can spot abnormal changes (including early signs of oral cancer) during regular visits — early detection significantly improves recovery chances

    • What is gum disease?

    Plaque buildup is inevitable regardless of brushing routine; if tartar isn't addressed it increases gum disease risk. The body naturally fights some plaque, but in some cases this response doesn't stop and goes on to break down bone and soft tissue — leading to loosening or, in the worst cases, tooth loss.

    What are the signs of gum disease?

    Not always obvious — signs include gums darkening in colour, inflammation/discomfort, teeth appearing longer or more spaced (roots becoming visible), bleeding gums (healthy gums don't bleed), increased tooth sensitivity, an unfamiliar/metallic taste, or bad breath. Early stage is called gingivitis. Gum disease can't be cured but can be managed — treatment involves a deep clean (debriding) plus long-term care advice; periodontitis beyond the gums may need a periodontist referral.

    Dental hygiene care for children

    Booking a hygiene appointment for children is a good opportunity to check their teeth and reinforce good habits early. Regular 6-month visits build lifelong habits and familiarity with the practice. Ages 7–14 are a higher-risk window as milk teeth are replaced by adult teeth; fissure sealants and fluoride treatments are available. The team keeps children's appointments short and comfortable.

    Extra tips for healthy teeth and gums

    • Watch diet — limit sugary food/drink and snacking
    • Brush twice daily; ask about interdental devices (brushes, floss, mouthwash)
    • Regularly inspect your mouth and get any changes checked promptly
