New patients

However long it's been, you're welcome back.

There's no judgment here for a gap since your last dental visit — only a clear path back to good oral health. Every new patient starts with a consultation built around your questions, your comfort, and no obligation to proceed with anything you're not ready for.

See What to Expect
Your first visit covers
  • 01 A conversation about your dental and general health — and any concerns you want to raise
  • 02 A full inspection of your teeth and gums, with intra-oral X-rays if needed
  • 03 A clear, no-obligation discussion of any treatment options
