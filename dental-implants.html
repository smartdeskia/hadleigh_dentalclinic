Dental Implants

Life-changing dentistry

Missing teeth affect more than the way you speak and eat — they can affect your confidence too. Dental implants are fast becoming the most popular way to replace missing teeth, permanently solving the problem by replacing them with a new porcelain implant designed to match your existing healthy teeth.

What are Dental Implants?

A dental implant has two parts: an abutment (a titanium screw that does the job of a natural tooth root, fitted into the jaw) and a dental crown (the visible replacement tooth, designed to match surrounding teeth, attached to the abutment). Together they form a permanent replacement that can last a lifetime.

Dental Implant Cost?

Prices generally start around £2,000 per tooth

For multiple missing teeth: a single implant abutment can sometimes support a 3-tooth implant bridge, replacing three missing teeth from one implant — worth explaining since it's a common misconception that cost scales 1:1 with missing teeth.

The process

Your 5-step journey to a new tooth

  • 01 Consultation — a half-hour session to meet the team, ask questions, understand cost, possibly get an X-ray for treatment options
  • 02 Treatment planning — detailed oral exam with the implantologist, bespoke treatment plan, possible hygienist sessions first to prepare gums
  • 03 Placement — the abutment is fitted under local or general anaesthetic (small hole made in the jawbone), a scan is sent to the lab to create the matching porcelain crown, then a 2–3 month healing period
  • 04 Fitting — once healed, the new crown is fitted (painless, no anaesthetic needed)
  • 05 Final check — a follow-up appointment to confirm the implant, jaw, and gums have healed properly
FAQ

More about Dental Implants

Because they are made from porcelain, dental implants are likely to be longer-lasting than natural teeth. Take good care of them and they will last a lifetime — it's the same as natural teeth, the better you look after them, the longer they will last.
