Spread the cost, interest-free.

Treatment from £500 can be spread over several months at 0% interest, with an easy in-practice application. Our finance plans are authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.

Ask About Finance
How payment works
  • We accept cash, cheque, and credit/debit card
  • Payment is due at the start of treatment, or per visit
  • Pre-arranged instalments are available for larger courses of treatment
  • You'll always receive an itemised estimate before any treatment begins
