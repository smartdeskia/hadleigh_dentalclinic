Teeth Whitening

Brighter, faster

Pola night tooth whitening, available in-surgery or as a home-use kit. In-surgery Pola Night whitening gives visible results quickly — Pola Office+ is one of the world's fastest whitening treatments, lightening enamel in as little as 30 minutes, often without needing a UV light.

Who is this suitable for?

Home kit option

Pola Day or Pola Night home kits are available, made from custom trays built from tooth impressions. Gel is worn 15–30 minutes a day; full effect can take up to 2 weeks, though many patients see a difference within a few days.

What does the gel taste like?

A spearmint-flavoured gel formulated to hydrate, soothe, and condition enamel — reducing sensitivity and plaque buildup rather than just whitening.

Philips Zoom

A visibly whiter smile after 3 days

Philips Zoom Teeth Whitening is a separate at-home whitening system:

  • Initial appointment to discuss goals and confirm suitability
  • Impressions taken for custom take-home trays
  • Trays checked for fit and comfort
  • Clinician demonstrates gel application

DayWhite / NiteWhite option

Choice of packs depending on lifestyle, whitening goals, and sensitivity level — ask the dentist or hygienist which is right for you.

NiteWhite specifics

Worn overnight (or minimum 4–6 hours); optimal results within 1–2 weeks; delivers whitening plus enamel protection.
