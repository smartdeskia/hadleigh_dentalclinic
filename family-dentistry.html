Family Dentistry

Care for all ages

Everyone is entitled to a beautiful, healthy smile and the confidence it can bring. A smile is one of the most important ways of communicating with the world. Our goal is to provide the highest level of patient care, making quality private dentistry accessible to everyone, in a relaxing, homely environment for patients of all ages.

NHS dental care

Dentistry is one of the few NHS services where a contribution toward the cost of care is required. This section explains what you may have to pay for NHS dental treatment.

NHS pricing bands
Emergency dental treatment — pain relief or a temporary filling£23.80
Band 1 course of treatment — examination, diagnosis (including X-rays), prevention advice, a scale and polish if clinically needed, preventative care (e.g. fluoride varnish or fissure sealant)£23.80
Band 2 course of treatment — everything in Band 1, plus further treatment such as fillings, root canal work, or tooth removal (not more complex Band 3 items)£65.20
Band 3 course of treatment — everything in Bands 1 and 2, plus crowns, dentures, bridges, and other laboratory work£282.80
