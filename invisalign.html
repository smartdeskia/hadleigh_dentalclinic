Invisalign

Platinum Elite Invisalign Provider

Uneven teeth making you afraid to smile? Invisalign could be the answer. Hadleigh Dental & Cosmetic Centre is a Platinum Elite Invisalign provider, having helped hundreds of patients achieve their dream smile with this world-renowned clear brace system. Invisalign uses see-through plastic aligners to gradually straighten teeth — practically invisible compared to traditional “train-track” braces, so treatment can start without anyone else noticing.

How it works

5 Steps of Invisalign

  • 01 Free Invisalign Consultation — visit the surgery, get guided through Invisalign options, ask about price/timeline/financing, then take time to think it over
  • 02 Invisalign Scan — once ready, a 3D visual simulation of the post-treatment smile is created from a scan; used to make the aligners if the patient proceeds
  • 03 Confirm Treatment & Get Started — payment arranged, first set of aligners ordered (arrive within about a week), fitting appointment booked
  • 04 Your Smile Starts to Show — a new set of aligners arrives every fortnight for the patient to fit themselves; teeth gradually move; regular check-ups monitor progress
  • 05 Final stage — Step 5 details to be confirmed from the official site. Contact us to learn about the final stage of your Invisalign journey.
