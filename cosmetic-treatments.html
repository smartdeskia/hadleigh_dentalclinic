Cosmetic Treatments

Facial aesthetics, with the same clinical care as your dentistry.

Dermal fillers, microdermabrasion and Botox — delivered by the same clinical team you already trust with your smile.

Dermal Fillers

Restylane dermal fillers are used for eye lines, mouth lines, cheek lines, chin indentations, frown lines and lip enhancement — long-lasting results without surgery.

Book a Consultation

Microdermabrasion

An exfoliation treatment for fine lines and wrinkles, sun damage, acne-prone skin, blackheads and whiteheads, age spots and hyperpigmentation, and oily or dry skin. Suitable for all skin types.

Book a Consultation

Botox

A non-surgical treatment for moderate-to-severe frown lines, typically for ages 18–65. The procedure takes around 10 minutes and needs no anaesthesia, with results appearing in 3–7 days and lasting up to around 4 months.

Not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or alongside certain nerve or muscle disorders — please disclose these before treatment.
