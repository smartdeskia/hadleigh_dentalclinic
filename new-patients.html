New Patients

However long it's been, you're welcome here.

There's no judgment for a gap since your last dental visit — only a clear, comfortable path back to good oral health.

Your first visit

What your consultation covers

Every new patient starts with a consultation designed around your questions and your comfort — including for patients who feel nervous about visiting the dentist. There's no obligation to proceed with any treatment discussed.

01

A conversation first

Questions about your dental and general health, and a chance to raise any concerns.

02

A thorough check

An initial inspection of your teeth and gums, with intra-oral X-rays if needed — results explained clearly.

03

Your options, your call

A discussion of treatment options where relevant, with no obligation to proceed with anything.

Good to know

Before you book

It's been a while — is that okay?

Completely. Getting your care back on track is the priority — not what happened in the past.

Nervous about the dentist?

Your consultation is designed to help you get comfortable with the practice before any treatment begins.

Only need a hygienist?

You don't need to be an existing patient — direct access to our hygienist is available without a referral.
